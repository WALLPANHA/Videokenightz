Put videos here to use them on the screen for every song.
Name them bg1.mp4, bg2.mp4, bg3.mp4 ... up to bg9 (.webm also works).
Then choose "My video folder" in Settings -> Playback & sound -> Background animation.
A random video from this folder plays (muted, looping) for each song.
For ONE song only, put its video in that song's folder instead and name it video.mp4.
Only use videos you have the right to use.
